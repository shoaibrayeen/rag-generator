# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Robotics. This handbook summarises the policies that apply to all
full-time employees working from the Bengaluru and Rotterdam offices.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours employees
may choose their own schedule as long as they complete 40 hours per week. Remote work is
allowed up to three days per week; managers may approve additional remote days for
caregiving or medical reasons.

## Leave policy

Employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly.
Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March.
Sick leave is 12 days per year and does not carry over. Parental leave is 26 weeks for the
primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, both fully paid.

## Expense reimbursement

Expenses must be submitted in the Concur portal within 30 days of being incurred.
Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to EUR 60 per day. Any single expense above
EUR 500 requires written pre-approval from a director. Receipts are mandatory for all
claims above EUR 25.

## Equipment

Every engineer receives a laptop refreshed every 3 years and a one-time home office
budget of EUR 750. Requests go through the IT helpdesk; delivery usually takes 5 business days.

## Code of conduct

Northwind expects respectful, inclusive behaviour. Concerns can be raised confidentially
with People Operations or through the anonymous ethics hotline at +31 20 555 0199.
