NORTHWIND ROBOTICS - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY (v2.3, effective 1 February 2026)

1. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor
authentication is mandatory for email, GitHub, AWS and the HR system.

2. Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled (FileVault or BitLocker) and screen lock
after 5 minutes of inactivity.

3. Production customer data may only be accessed through the bastion host "gatekeeper".
Direct database connections from laptops are prohibited.

4. Security incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of
discovery. The on-call security engineer can be paged via the #sec-oncall Slack channel.

5. Third-party vendors handling personal data require a signed Data Processing Agreement
and an annual SOC 2 Type II report before onboarding.

6. Access reviews are performed quarterly by team leads; unused accounts are disabled
after 90 days of inactivity.
