Terminal statuses (no further change): everything except QUEUED and PROCESSING. Poll GET /api/jobs/{job_id} until the status is terminal. Retry (POST /api/documents/{id}/retry, the retry button on the home page, /documents and the show page, or rag retry) is offered for every document that is not COMPLETED or DUPLICATE; the server applies the rules in the last column.
